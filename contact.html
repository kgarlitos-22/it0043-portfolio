Want to know more about me?

Art wasn’t always my main passion. I became more interested in it when I started working on film projects, especially directing and screenwriting. I’m still learning the technical side of things, including 3D, but I’m having fun exploring and improving along the way. I’m drawn to stories that reflect real life and leave room for interpretation. I especially enjoy open endings that make audiences debate what happened and form their own conclusions. I hope to keep writing and conceptualizing stories that express raw emotions. One project I’m especially proud of is my one-act play, The Prodigal Son, because I love how its plot twist comes together.

Trivia Time!

In my free time, I enjoy reading manhwa and binge-watching movies and series, especially critically acclaimed films. My personal favorite movie is Call Me by Your Name. I’m most familiar with Photoshop, and I’m still learning more about 3D.
